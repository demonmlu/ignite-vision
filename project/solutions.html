Tailored Solutions for Every Marketing Challenge

Whether you are an e-commerce brand scaling influencer campaigns or an agency managing multiple clients, Ignite Vision adapts to your workflow.

SOLUTION 01

E-Commerce Brands

The Challenge: E-commerce brands need a large number of influencers and promotion channels to sustain sales velocity, but sourcing and managing them individually is operationally impossible at scale.

Ignite Vision’s AI simultaneously analyses thousands of potential influencers and channels matched against your product category, price point, and target demographic. Automated outreach and campaign management enable you to run hundreds of concurrent influencer and channel partnerships without proportional headcount increases.

Key Benefits

  • Speed in scaling partnerships and order volume
  • AI-optimised partnership invitation based on content-performance correlation
  • AI monitoring of compliance
  • Real-time sales attribution
SOLUTION 02

Retail Brands

The Challenge: Retail brands face uncertainty about which influencers authentically align with their brand identity, leading to partnerships that feel inauthentic and fail to resonate with target audiences.

Starry AI engine performs deep semantic analysis of both brand values and influencer content to surface creators whose audience demographics, content tone, and engagement patterns genuinely align with your brand positioning. Every recommendation comes with a transparent fit score and supporting data.

Key Benefits

  • Brand-value alignment scoring for every influencer recommendation
  • Audience overlap analysis to eliminate redundant partnerships
  • Competitive intelligence on influencer strategies within your retail category
  • Seasonal campaign planning with predictive demand modelling

  • Muti-lingual campaign management and cultural adaptation support
