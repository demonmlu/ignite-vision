Privacy Policy

We run an AI matching engine over millions of creator profiles and handle briefs from brands across six markets. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Version 1.0

We do not sell personal data. We collect the minimum needed to answer your brief, run campaigns, prevent fraud and meet our legal obligations — and we tell creators and channel partners how their profiles are used.

我们不出售个人数据。我们仅收集回复需求、执行活动、防范欺诈与履行法定义务所必需的最少数据，并向达人与渠道伙伴说明其档案的使用方式。

Who we are

Ignite Vision Group is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. We are headquartered in Hong Kong and operate offices in Guangzhou, Taipei, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta. Our platforms are ChineseAN (affiliate and channel partnerships) and Starry (AI influencer marketing). Where Group entities jointly determine the purposes of processing, they act as joint controllers and you may exercise your rights against any of them.

Ignite Vision Group 为本政策所述个人数据的控制者，总部位于香港，并在广州、台北、新加坡、吉隆坡与雅加达设有办事处。我们的平台为 ChineseAN（联盟与渠道合作）与 Starry（AI 达人营销）。若集团内实体共同决定处理目的，则构成共同控制者，您可向其中任一实体行使权利。

  • 提交需求或联系办事处的人士；
  • 客户、广告主、代理商员工、发布商与渠道伙伴；

  • 求职者与商务联系人。

Data we collect

Information you give us

Name, company, work email, and optional phone number or messaging ID; the content of your brief, including brand, target markets, objective, budget range, timeline and platform interest; campaign and billing contacts; and, where you join a platform, creator onboarding details, channel URLs and payout information.

Information collected automatically

IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and browser type, pages viewed, referrer, timestamps, language preference and error logs. Your preferences and unfinished briefs are stored locally in your browser (see section 06).

Creator and channel data

Public handles, display names, profile URLs, follower and subscriber counts, content topics and language, publishing frequency, engagement metrics, market, and aggregated audience characteristics. Sources are public platform pages, partner data feeds, licensed providers, and creators or publishers themselves.

Information from others

Referrals from agencies and partners, verification and anti-fraud signals, and business contact details supplied by your employer.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of data — such as health, religion, biometrics or political opinions — or data of children. Where such data appears in public creator content ingested into our databases, we filter it out and exclude it from matching.

您主动提供

姓名、公司、工作邮箱，以及可选的电话号码或即时通讯账号；需求内容，包括品牌、目标市场、活动目标、预算区间、时间与意向平台；活动与账单联系人；加入平台时的达人入驻资料、频道链接与结算信息。

自动收集

IP 地址及由其推断的大致位置、设备与浏览器类型、浏览页面、来源页、时间戳、语言偏好与错误日志。您的偏好设置与未完成的草稿保存在浏览器本地（见第 06 节）。

达人与渠道数据

公开账号、显示名称、主页链接、粉丝与订阅数、内容主题与语言、发布频率、互动指标、所在市场，以及聚合的受众特征。来源为平台公开页面、伙伴数据源、获授权供应商，以及达人或发布商本人。

来自第三方

代理商与伙伴的推荐、核验与反欺诈信号，以及您雇主提供的商务联系信息。

我们不会有意收集特殊类别数据（如健康、宗教、生物识别或政治观点）或儿童数据。若纳入数据库的公开达人内容中出现该等数据，我们会予以过滤，并排除在匹配之外。

AI processing and profiling

Starry analyses content semantics, audience characteristics, engagement patterns and brand-affinity signals to produce alignment scores, shortlists and predicted outcomes. This is profiling in the technical sense: it evaluates suitability for a campaign, not a person's character or creditworthiness.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. Campaign selection, contracting and payment always involve human review. If a score or ranking materially affects you, you may request an explanation of the factors involved and ask for human reconsideration through the contact route in section 11.

Starry 分析内容语义、受众特征、互动模式与品牌契合信号，生成契合度评分、短名单与预测结果。此属技术意义上的画像：评估的是与活动的适配性，而非个人品行或信用状况。

我们不会仅依据自动化处理作出对您产生法律或类似重大影响的决定。活动选角、签约与付款始终包含人工复核。若某项评分或排序对您有实质影响，您可通过第 11 节的联系渠道，要求说明所涉因素并申请人工重新评估。

Cookies and local storage

This site uses a small number of strictly necessary browser storage items so that language choice, drafts and preferences persist. Non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are enabled only with your consent where consent is required.

You can clear or block storage in your browser settings. The site will still work, but your language choice and drafts will not persist. Where consent is required in your region, a preference control is presented before any optional technology is set.

本网站使用少量严格必要的浏览器存储项，以保留语言选择、草稿与偏好。非必要的分析或广告技术，仅在法律要求同意的情形下取得您的同意后方才启用。

您可在浏览器设置中清除或阻止存储。网站仍可正常使用，但语言选择与草稿将不再保留。在您所在地区要求同意的情形下，我们会在设置任何可选技术前提供偏好控制。

Security

We apply role-based access control, least-privilege provisioning, encryption in transit, logging and monitoring, vendor due diligence, and fraud detection developed from our Quality Vision anti-abuse work. Staff are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive data protection training.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant regulators within the timelines required by applicable law.

我们实施基于角色的访问控制、最小权限配置、传输加密、日志与监控、供应商尽职调查，以及源自 Quality Vision 反滥用实践的欺诈检测。员工负有保密义务并接受数据保护培训。

没有任何系统是绝对安全的。若发生影响您权利的泄露事件，我们将在适用法律要求的时限内通知您及相关监管机构。

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent, receive a copy in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and complain to a regulator. Creators may ask us to correct or remove a profile even if they have never registered with us.

视您所在地法律，您可能享有查阅副本、更正、删除、限制或反对处理、撤回同意、以结构化、通用且机器可读的格式获取副本，以及向监管机构投诉的权利。达人即使从未在我们平台注册，亦可要求更正或删除其档案。

Marketing choices

Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link, and you may opt out at any time by writing to us. Opting out does not affect service messages about an active campaign, an invoice or a security notice. Where local law requires prior consent for electronic marketing, we obtain it before sending.

每封营销邮件均附有退订链接，您也可随时致函我们退订。退订不影响与进行中活动、发票或安全通知有关的服务类信息。若当地法律要求电子营销须事先取得同意，我们会在发送前取得。

Children and third-party links

Our services are business-to-business and are not directed at anyone under 18. If we learn that we hold a child's data without appropriate authorisation, we will delete it. Our pages link to social networks, messaging channels and partner sites whose own privacy policies govern their processing. We are not responsible for their practices.

我们的服务面向企业客户，不针对未满 18 岁人士。若我们发现未经授权持有儿童数据，将予以删除。本页面含有指向社交网络、消息渠道与伙伴网站的链接，其处理行为受各自隐私政策约束。我们不对其做法负责。
