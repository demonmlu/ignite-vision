The Ignite Vision Platform

Two Purpose-Built Sub-Platforms

Complementary platforms addressing the two largest channels in the influencer economy.

KOL Marketing

Starry is Ignite Vision's KOL marketing platform, leveraging proprietary AI to identify, evaluate, and engage the most suitable key opinion leaders for any brand or campaign. It combines deep influencer intelligence with end-to-end campaign automation — covering KOL discovery, content collaboration, communication management, and post-campaign analytics — so brands can launch data-driven influencer campaigns at scale with measurable, predictable outcomes.

Affiliate Marketing

ChineseAN is Ignite Vision's dedicated affiliate marketing platform, purpose-built to connect brands with a vast network of affiliate partners across APAC and cross-border e-commerce ecosystems. It streamlines the entire affiliate lifecycle — from partner discovery and onboarding to real-time performance tracking, commission management, and ROI attribution — typically working on performance-based pricing models such as CPS, CPA, CPI, and CPL.
