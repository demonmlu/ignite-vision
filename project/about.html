About Ignite Vision

Redefining Influencer Marketing Through Artificial Intelligence

Ignite Vision's AI platform identifies, engages, and activates the ideal influencer mix for your brand from a database of over 2 million creators and 60,000 channel partnerships across Asia-Pacific and beyond.

Our Mission

To make influencer marketing predictable, measurable, and scalable for every brand.

We believe every brand deserves access to the right influencers, and every campaign should deliver provable results. Ignite Vision's AI platform eliminates guesswork and manual overhead, empowering marketers to focus on strategy and creativity while our technology handles discovery, engagement, analytics, and optimisation.

Our Vision

The operating system for the global influencer economy.

We envision a future where brand-influencer partnerships are as data-driven, transparent, and efficient as programmatic advertising. Ignite Vision aims to be the foundational platform that powers this transformation across every market, language, and content vertical in the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

Our Story

Built by Marketers, Powered by AI

Ignite Vision was founded by a team of marketing veterans and technology leaders who experienced firsthand the inefficiencies of influencer marketing at scale. Brands were spending months on manual influencer research, outreach, and reporting, only to see unpredictable results and missed market windows.

Starting in Hong Kong, the team built an AI-powered platform that could analyse millions of influencer profiles, predict campaign outcomes, and automate the entire workflow from discovery to ROI measurement. What began as a solution for the team's own agency challenges quickly evolved into a platform serving e-commerce brands, retail giants, China outbound marketers, and agencies across the region.

Today, Ignite Vision operates from six offices across the Asia-Pacific region, with a growing network of over 60,000 channel partnerships and data on more than 2 million influencers. The company continues to invest heavily in AI research, regional expansion, and ecosystem development to serve the fast-growing influencer economy.
